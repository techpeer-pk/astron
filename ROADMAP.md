# Astron — Project Roadmap

## Company Overview
Astron is an IT solutions provider offering end-to-end technology products and services across:
- IT Infrastructure (servers, desktops, laptops)
- Networking & Structured Cabling
- Power Backup (UPS systems)
- Surveillance & Security (CCTV, access control)
- Printers, Scanners, Barcode & RFID
- Software Solutions (ERP, business applications, software licensing)
- Professional & Managed Services (SLA-based support, MSP)
- Web Development services

---

## Phase 3 — Content & SEO
**Goal:** Rank for relevant IT services keywords in the target market.

- [ ] Write SEO-optimized page copy for all service pages
- [ ] Compress and properly alt-tag all images
- [ ] Set up Google Search Console and submit sitemap
- [ ] Identify top 20 target keywords (e.g., "IT infrastructure solutions", "UPS suppliers", "CCTV installation", "ERP software", "managed IT services")
- [ ] Publish 4–6 foundational blog articles covering each service vertical
- [ ] Build internal linking structure across service pages
- [ ] Set up local SEO (Google Business Profile, NAP consistency)

**Deliverables:** On-page SEO complete, Google Business Profile live, initial blog posts published
